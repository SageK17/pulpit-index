0.241	As this sacrifice changes him, he wants to do good to his neighbor, just as good has been done to him.
0.242	He is even willing to risk his life for this. He knows he has been rescued from eternal death, and he knows that the death of the body has no power over his eternal life.
0.243	But a heart that does not take hold of Christ’s sacrifice has no faith. It does not feel God’s love or eternal life.
0.244	34. John uses an example that is simple enough for anyone to understand. From it we can tell that a person who fails in small duties will also fail in big ones.
0.245	The apostle says this: suppose someone has this world’s goods and sees his neighbor in need. He could help without hurting himself. Yet he shuts his heart against that neighbor and does not give even the smallest act of love. How can God’s love live in him, when he cares about it so little that he will not spare a penny for his needy brother?
0.246	Then how could we expect him to do something greater—even to give his life for his brother?
0.247	What right does such a person have to brag that Christ gave his life for him and rescued him from death? How can he even know it?
0.248	35. How often we find people like this!
0.249	They have this world’s goods and are able to help the needy, but they shut their hearts to those in trouble, just as the greedy rich man did to poor Lazarus.
0.250	In the courts of emperors, among kings, princes, and lords, where will we find anyone who reaches out a helping hand to the needy Church? Who gives her even a crust of bread to support the poor, the ministry, and the schools, or for any of her other needs?
0.251	How would they do at the greater duty of giving their lives for their brothers, and especially for the Christian Church?
0.252	Notice the terrible judgment: in God’s eyes, people who have no brotherly love are murderers, and they cannot have eternal life.
0.253	36. But people who are just selfish might almost escape our blame, compared with others who are far worse. These others not only close their wallets to the poor. They also shamelessly and by force take what belongs to their needy neighbor. They do it by cheating, fraud, oppression, and extortion. They take from the Church the property that rightly belongs to her and was set aside especially for her. You could say they snatch the bread right out of her mouth.
0.254	The papist mob today is guilty of this sin. But so are many who want to be known as evangelical. They commit the same fraud with the parish lands and the general property of the Church. On top of that, they bully and torment the poor ministers like tyrants.
0.255	But oh, how heavy and terrible is the judgment coming for those who would not even give Christ the Lord a cup of cold water when he was thirsty!
0.256	IV. “My little children, let us not love with words or with our tongues. Let us love with actions and in truth.”
0.257	37. The world and false Christians pretend to have great love when they talk. But in practice, when love should show itself in actions, it turns out to be fake.
0.258	So John warns us: if our love is not strong enough to lead us to give our lives for our brothers, then no matter how much we say we believe in Christ, our love is only an empty show. It is a false front that we fool ourselves with. With it we stay in unbelief and death, and we end up worse off than people who know nothing at all about the Gospel.
0.259	So whoever wants to be safe and show that he is a Christian should remember to prove it by his deeds and actions.
0.260	Then people will know that he does not follow the devil as a murderer and liar, the way others do.
0.261	Instead, they will know that he truly holds on to God’s Word with his heart, because he has passed from death to life.
