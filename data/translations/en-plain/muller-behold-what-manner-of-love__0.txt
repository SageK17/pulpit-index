0.1	“Look! See what kind of love the Father has given us: that we should be called the sons of God! That is why the world does not know us, because it did not know Him.
0.2	Dear friends, we are the sons of God now. What we will be has not yet been shown. But we know that when He appears, we will be like Him. For we will see Him as He is.
0.3	And every man who has this hope in him makes himself pure, just as He is pure.”
0.4	1 John 3:1–3
0.5	We need to be reminded of the truth in these verses again and again. That is why God points us especially to this small passage. He puts the word “Look!” in front of it!
0.6	It is as if He were saying: “My dear children, all of God’s revealed will is important. All of the Holy Scriptures, which I have put into your hands, should be thought about, pondered and read from time to time. But there are certain passages you especially need to read from time to time. You especially need to ponder them from time to time. This is because you are spiritually weak, and because of the spiritual difficulties you face as you pass through this valley of tears. So, because you are weak, I point you to passages like these.”
0.7	So now, let us ponder – really ponder – the truth in the short statement of these three verses.
0.8	“Look!” Our Father would say to us, “Look at it carefully. Think about it. Pray over it again and again. Take it to heart even more than you have done up to now!”
0.9	“See what kind of love the Father has given us, that we should be called the sons of God.”
0.10	That is the special point we are asked to look at first.
0.11	Suppose God had only forgiven us, so that we were not punished for our countless sins, and had done nothing more. Even that would have been amazing grace. But He has done far, far, FAR more than this.
0.12	That is why it says, “What kind of love.”
0.13	This is how great it is. The Lord has passed over our countless sins and forgiven every one of them. We will not be treated as even one thousandth of our sins deserve. No, we will not be punished for even one single sin, whether in action, word or thought. But that is not all: He makes us His own children. He takes us into the Heavenly Family.
0.14	By nature we are rebels against Him. We despise His love. We do not care about Him in the least. We show this total dislike and disregard for God every day, by going our own way and doing the things He hates. Yet we are forgiven. We will not be punished for a single sin out of the many tens of thousands we have committed, whether in action, word, thought, feeling, desire or inclination. More than that, we are made His own children and taken into His family. And this is not only in name, but in reality.
0.15	By the power of the Holy Spirit, through faith in the Gospel, He gives us new birth. He makes us a new creation in Christ. He makes us His very own children.
0.16	He does not only call us His children. He makes us His very own children.
0.17	He gives us spiritual life, heavenly life, and so He makes us His very own children.
0.18	That is the amazing grace we should think deeply about.
0.19	God calls us to ponder it. We should not pass it by lightly or think little of it. We should think very, very, very much about it. We should never let it slip out of our minds until at last we reach our home in glory!
0.20	This is the “kind of love” that “the Father has given us.”
0.21	O Lord! Help us to think about it a thousand times more than we have so far.
0.22	O Lord! By the power of Your Holy Spirit, help us to take it to heart a thousand times more than we have so far. As we think about it, pray over it, and take it to heart much more than before, fill our hearts with love and thankfulness to You as never before!
0.23	Oh, please let it be so, for Jesus Christ’s sake, we beg You.
0.24	“Look! See what kind of love the Father has given us.”
0.25	Now here is a good practical point.
0.26	“Given us.” And then to be able to say, “Given me.”
0.27	By the grace of God, I can say that.
0.28	By the grace of God, a great many people here can say it too.
0.29	But can everyone say it?
0.30	This is what I long for. This is what I pray God will give to everyone here: that each one will be able to say, “He has given it to me.”
0.31	Oh, how happy this would make us! How heavenly-minded it would make us! How dead to the world it would make us!
0.32	And in some small way, it would make us more like Christ than we have been so far!
0.33	“Given us.” Think of it: we sinners, just as we are, rebels by nature, should be called the sons of God! More literally and correctly, the children of God. For this blessing is not only for men who believe, but also for women who believe. It is for everyone who loves the Lord Jesus and trusts Him to save their souls!
0.34	So: that we should be called the children of God.
0.35	How precious! It is a blessing too great for words that we belong to the Heavenly Family!
0.36	By nature, every one of us is just like the Jews to whom the Lord Jesus once said, “Your father is the devil” – “for you do his works. You act the way he thinks. You act like your father, the devil.”
0.37	This was not only true of the Israelites the Lord was speaking to. It is true of us too, as long as we do not believe in the Lord Jesus.
0.38	We may call ourselves children of God. We may call God our Father. But it is not true as long as we are not trusting the Lord Jesus Christ to save our souls. But the moment we believe in the Lord Jesus Christ, everything is forgiven. It does not matter how long or how much we have lived in sin, how many kinds of sin we have committed, or how great they were. We receive the Gospel and are given new life. We are born again. Then we truly are the children of God, and we belong to the Heavenly Family!
0.39	Then, through the apostle, the Holy Spirit adds, “That is why the world does not know us, because it did not know Him.”
0.40	The children are not known because the Father is not known.
0.41	As long as people do not believe in the Lord Jesus Christ, they do not know God’s children as children of God!
0.42	They may know their names. They may know what work they do, where they live and how they dress. They may know other such things that belong to the outward person and to this present life. But people who are not converted themselves do not know believers in Jesus as God’s children. The reason is given here: “Because they do not know the Father” of the children.
0.43	They do not know Almighty God and the Lord Jesus Christ. So they do not really and truly know God’s children as children of God.
0.44	Ungodly people cannot see the divine life.
0.45	“Dear friends, we are the sons of God now.” Or: “Dear friends, we are the children of God now.” For the same correction needs to be made here.
0.46	“Now!” We should especially think about this little word “now.” We should hold on to it and ponder it deeply.
0.47	This is what it means. We are still in the body. We are still weak, troubled by many weaknesses in many ways, and very ignorant. To a greater or lesser degree, all true believers in the Lord Jesus Christ are in this state of weakness and helplessness. Even so, we are children of God. We are not all like John, who wrote this letter, or like Paul, or like Peter. We have many weaknesses and frailties, failures and shortcomings. But as surely as we trust in the Lord Jesus Christ to save our souls, we are already, even now in this body, really and truly the children of God!
0.48	What a precious truth this is!
0.49	We must hold on to this little word “now” by faith. We must ponder it in our hearts again and again and again. We must not let it go. And we must not think that we only become children of God when we die, or when the Lord Jesus Christ comes again.
0.50	No, we are already the children of God now.
0.51	As all of you can see at once, even the youngest believers, this means we have a Father in heaven. And this Father of ours in heaven is none other than Almighty God, the God who can do everything, for whom nothing is impossible.
0.52	See how precious this is.
0.53	Our Father can do everything!
0.54	So He is infinitely wise. He is infinitely rich. He is infinitely powerful. And His heart is full of infinite love for the weakest and feeblest of God’s children.
0.55	So suppose I am in pain in my body. Then let me go to my Heavenly Father and talk to Him about it as simply as a child. Let me ask Him, if it is for His glory and for my real good, benefit and blessing, to kindly take the pain away completely or else make it less. Or, if the pain needs to go on, let me ask Him to hold me up under it, so that it does not overwhelm me. Above all, let me ask that I will not fret, complain and grumble. Instead, I want to receive it from His loving hand as a blessing given to me, which in the end must turn out for my good.
0.56	If we are going through a family trial, we should say to ourselves: “This family trial is very heavy for me to bear. More than that, it will be too much for me if I have to carry it myself. I will put the matter into the hands of my Heavenly Father. I will ask Him to kindly take the trial away, if that is for His honor and glory and for my real blessing.”
0.57	He is able to do it, because He can do everything. And He has shown how deep His love is: He did not spare His one and only Son, but gave Him up for us all.
0.58	Then again, people in jobs, in business or in a profession run into difficulties connected with their trade, their business or their profession.
0.59	Now the important thing is not to carry the burden ourselves, but to throw it onto the Lord.
0.60	He is willing to hold us up and willing to help us. When we do this, we go through life peacefully and quietly. If we throw the burden onto Him and do not try to carry it ourselves, we will not tend to fret, complain, grumble, or be unhappy with the way God deals with us!
0.61	That is exactly what we should do. And it is one of the many reasons why the Holy Spirit says here, “We are the children of God now.” That is, while we are still in the body, still surrounded by trials and difficulties, and still finding that struggle is more or less part of our life.
0.62	Oh, this precious little word, “NOW.”
0.63	It holds a great deal of deeply instructive, teaching truth.
0.64	Also, we now face the spiritual struggle. Our natural evil tendencies are still in us, even though we have been born again.
0.65	The old nature has not been taken away. It remains in us, just as it was before we believed in the Lord Jesus Christ.
0.66	It is true that we have been given new life. We are born again. It is true that we have spiritual life. But it is also true that the old nature is not dead. It is still in us. It can only be kept down by prayer, by meditating on and pondering the Word of God, and by using our faith all the time.
0.67	So when trials come because of the old, evil, corrupt nature in us, we should lay the matter simply before God. We should say, “My Heavenly Father, I have no strength in myself. But You have almighty power. Your heart is full of love for me. You have proved Your amazing love for me by bringing me to Jesus, and by giving Jesus for me, a poor, miserable, guilty sinner.
0.68	Now help me in this spiritual struggle of mine.
0.69	Oh, do not let the devil’s cunning overpower me because I am spiritually weak.
0.70	Oh, help me! Help me!
0.71	Help me!” And what will we find?
0.72	The Lord is willing to help us!
0.73	The Lord is willing to help us!!
0.74	I can assure my young brothers and sisters in Christ how He has helped me, times without number, for seventy-one years and five months now. He helped me especially when the divine life had just begun in me.
0.75	As an unconverted young man, I had formed evil habits. I had lived an ungodly life until the end of my twentieth year. Because of this, I found it extremely hard to overcome those evil tendencies I had picked up. And that was even though I really was a child of God, and I hated sin and loved holiness.
0.76	It seemed to me: “Oh, it will never change. My prayer will never be answered.”
0.77	But by the grace of God, I rolled my burden onto Him, and came to Him again and again.
0.78	And so, little by little, it happened. It was only little by little; it took some time. But these natural tendencies were overcome, and God helped me.
0.79	I mention this especially to comfort and encourage young believers in Christ who have recently been converted. I want them not to despair, but to expect help from God. For He is able and willing to help them.
0.80	As long as we go to the Lord in our weakness and helplessness, we will never, never be overcome. And because we are the children of God now, glory will be ours in the end.
0.81	We do not become children of God only when this life is over. No! We are still in the body and still here on earth. We are still very weak, helpless and ignorant about many things. The devil still has power over us; he has not yet been thrown into the bottomless pit. Yet even now we are the children of God, and we will get help from God just as we need it.
0.82	Oh, how comforting this word is.
0.83	So let us keep pondering it and not lose sight of it.
0.84	“Dear friends, now” – in weakness; “now,” while the devil still has so much power; “now,” while we are so very ignorant – “we are the children of God, and what we will be has not yet been shown.”
0.85	We are now children of God. As His children we have many privileges. We may go to God at any time for guidance, advice, help and strength. We may go to Him to be rescued from difficulties, and to be supplied with what we need in earthly and spiritual things. Yet great as the privileges we already enjoy are, they are small compared with what we will have later.
0.86	So we must also think about this. On the one hand, we should never lose sight of this word “now.” But on the other hand, we should not forget what is written here: “What we will be has not yet been shown.”
0.87	And what is it that will be shown later?
0.88	What will I have later, poor, worthless worm that I am?
0.89	What will I, an ignorant person, know then?
0.90	What will be found in me later – in me, who am weak, who go astray and who fall?
0.91	Oh, this is a deeply important thought.
0.92	“What we will be has not yet been shown.” It has not yet been made known what we will be.
0.93	Oh, what will it be like for the body?
0.94	What will it be like for the soul?
0.95	What will it be like for our knowledge?
0.96	What will it be like for our spiritual strength?
0.97	What will it be like for our service for the Lord?
0.98	Oh, what will it be like in every way?
0.99	We will be given an eternal blessing, from then on and forever!
0.100	“What we will be has not yet been shown. But we know that when He appears, we will be like Him, for we will see Him as He is.”
0.101	When Jesus appears, we will be like Him. We will be like Him in His glorified body, the body He has had since He rose from the dead.
0.102	Some of us are often in pain in our bodies. Or we feel our weakness and frailty, which keep reminding us that we are not yet home and do not yet have the glorified body. Oh, how precious it is to think that a day is coming when there will not be the slightest trace of discomfort, pain, suffering, weakness or helplessness! For we will have a glorified body, exactly like the body the Lord Jesus Christ has had since His own resurrection.
0.103	What a precious, bright, glorious future this is!
0.104	In this body we will know nothing of tiredness, because it will be the same kind of body the Lord Jesus Christ has had since His resurrection.
0.105	Right now we may be able to work joyfully and gladly for eight, ten or twelve hours a day, sometimes fourteen or even sixteen. But in the end weakness comes, because we are still in this lowly body and not yet in the glorified body.
0.106	But then, in the life to come, we will work twenty-four hours, and the next day the same, and the next day the same. So we will be able to work seven times twenty-four hours every week. And we will be able to work the whole day, thirty days every month.
0.107	And so it will go on, month after month, year after year, one hundred years after another, one thousand years after another, one million years after another, all through eternity.
0.108	Work, work, work! Constant work for God’s glory in this glorified body of ours!
0.109	Oh, what bright, blessed, glorious prospects these are, if our hearts take them in.
0.110	Oh, how glad it makes us to think of working for God all through eternity without the slightest weakness, tiredness or suffering!
0.111	But this is only one part of it.
0.112	Here is the other part.
0.113	We will be perfectly holy, as the Lord Jesus Christ was during the thirty-three and a half years He was on earth!
0.114	Nothing He did was ever the least bit wrong. Nothing He said was ever the least bit against the mind of God. Nothing in all His thoughts, desires or wishes was ever the least bit against the mind of God.
0.115	Everything fully agreed with the mind of God during the whole time the blessed Savior was here on earth!
0.116	And that is how it will be with us.
0.117	We who are weak and feeble will not always be weak and feeble. We will be holy, spotless, pure and lovely.
0.118	Yes, lovely! Oh, how lovely!
0.119	Because the beauty of Christ is put on us!
0.120	Oh, how precious these words are! And oh, if we kept them in mind more and took them in, how our hearts would be full of peace and joy all day long, every day.
0.121	That is why it says, “What we will be has not yet been shown. But we know that when He appears, we will be like Him.”
0.122	We will be like Him!
0.123	Notice the reason why!
0.124	“We will be like Him, for we will see Him as He is!”
0.125	More correctly: “For we will see Him just as He is.”
0.126	That means we will know the Lord Jesus Christ perfectly, in all His work and in all His offices. We will not just know Him as our Judge.
0.127	That is how ungodly people will have to get to know Him.
0.128	Every person who does not believe in Christ will know Him as their Judge. But we will know Him as our Savior, our Brother, our Friend, our Husband and our Bridegroom.
0.129	We will know Him in every one of the offices He holds for the good of God’s Church. And the more we know the Lord now, the more we are shaped to be like Him. We become more and more like Him, even while we are still in the body.
0.130	The better we know the Lord Jesus Christ, the more like Him we are. And then, in glory, we will know that Blessed One perfectly, and we will be perfectly like Him!
0.131	What a bright and blessed future this is!
0.132	So our service all through eternity will not only be free from weakness and tiredness, pain and suffering. It will also be completely in line with the mind of God, completely in the same spirit in which the Lord Jesus Christ worked while He was in the body here on earth!
0.133	We have precious, bright, glorious prospects!
0.134	The world does not care about the things of God for just this reason: it is so ignorant, so completely ignorant, of all the glorious things that belong to the believer in Christ. If people knew the blessed position and share of a child of God, everyone would seek to know Him, everyone would care about Him, and everyone would believe in Him.
0.135	Now the last point: “And every man who has this hope in Him makes himself pure, just as He is pure.”
0.136	First of all, the more correct literal meaning is this: “Every man” – that is, “everyone” – “who has this hope in Him.”
0.137	It does not mean, “has this hope in himself.”
0.138	That is not what it means. It means “has this hope about the Lord Jesus Christ”: the hope that, through faith in Him, he will be perfectly like Christ in heaven.
0.139	“Everyone who has this hope in Him, or about Him, makes himself pure, just as He is pure.”
0.140	In other words, truth always tends to make us more holy, in every way. So here we repeat that statement again.
0.141	Whoever has this hope about the Lord Jesus, the hope of being made like Him in body and soul – everyone who has this hope about Him makes himself pure.
0.142	This hope tends to make us more and more holy. For as we get to know Christ and see what God has given us in Him, the more clearly, plainly and fully we understand this, the more our holiness grows. Then we will not be satisfied with anything less than this: to keep on becoming more and more like Christ.
0.143	We do not reach it fully while we are in this body. Yet this will be our aim more and more, more and more, more and more: to be like Christ.
0.144	We are not satisfied just to have power over our natural, gross sins. That does not satisfy us. We are only satisfied when we become more and more like Christ in spirit and in mind, gentle and loving.
0.145	Oh, how we try more and more to reach this! We long for it more and more. We try in every way to become like Christ.
0.146	We will never reach it fully while we are in this body. Yet no one can say how far we may become like Christ, even while we are in the body.
0.147	So let this be the great lesson we learn this evening. Because we are already children of God now, we can receive blessing, wonderful blessing, from our Heavenly Father. And because of the future we have before us, no one can say how far we may become like Christ.
0.148	Now, you who do not believe in Christ: will you always keep going the way you have gone until now?
0.149	Will nothing change?
0.150	If you keep going on the broad road, final destruction will be your end!
0.151	Do you long to spend a happy eternity with the Lord in heaven?
0.152	Oh, what a joy it will be for many in the end to see that not one of all the people here is missing in heaven!
0.153	I have a personal interest in your spiritual welfare. Because I love your soul, I long to meet you in heaven. And oh, how it would add to our joy and delight in heaven at last to find that not one is missing! How it would add to our joy to find that this little meditation of ours on the evening of April 11, 1897, was not in vain.
0.154	Oh, how precious to find it so in the end!
0.155	And now, is there anyone here who says, “I will still have the world. I will still try to enjoy the world”?
0.156	You will not be happy with this decision. You can be quite sure of that.
0.157	Oh, I tried your ways for twenty years and five weeks. All I ever got was disappointment and more guilt on my conscience.
0.158	But when I found Jesus, real happiness came.
0.159	Oh, such happiness that I cannot describe it!
0.160	That was at the beginning of November 1825. I have felt it ever since. The only difference is that the happiness has grown more and more, more and more.
0.161	And that is what God is willing to give you. For I think there is no one here who is a greater sinner than I was. I was only twenty years old, yet God gave me this wonderful blessing. And what He did for me, what He did for Paul and what He did for other sinners, He is willing to do for anyone else.
0.162	So, oh, let it be Christ you choose, and not the world any longer! For the world will never prove real. If you keep on with it, it will bring damnation, and only damnation.
0.163	May God give His blessing, for Jesus Christ’s sake.
