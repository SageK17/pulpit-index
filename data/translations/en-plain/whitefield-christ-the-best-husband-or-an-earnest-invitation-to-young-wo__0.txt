0.1	This psalm is called the song of loves. These are the purest and most spiritual loves, the dearest and most delightful. They are the loves between Christ, the one who is loved, and his church, which is his bride. First, the psalm shows the Lord Jesus Christ in his majesty, power, and divine nature, and in his truth, gentleness, and fairness. Then it shows the bride: her jewelry, her friends, her servants, and the children who come after her. And it shows how lovely and beautiful both of them are.
0.2	After describing Christ, the psalm invites the human race to his wedding, and it calls them “daughter.” So it speaks especially to you, my dear sisters, because you are daughters of the human race. But that does not leave out the sons of the human race.
0.3	So now I will look at these words as spoken especially to you. They teach this truth:
0.4	The Lord Jesus Christ invites the daughters of the human race to be his bride. He greatly desires their beauty. He means those who forget their own people and their father’s house, who listen, think it over, and lean toward his invitation, and who join themselves to him in this relationship.
0.5	I will show you these things:
0.6	I. How Christ joins himself in marriage to people, and especially to women.
0.7	The Lord Jesus Christ joins himself in marriage to people here in this world. But the public wedding ceremony will wait until the last day. On that day his bride will be brought to him in white robes, dressed in perfect righteousness. My dear sisters, that clothing will be richer and finer than any of your needlework. The wedding feast will be held in his Father’s house in heaven. Then his people will be welcomed into the closest, warmest embrace of his love.
0.8	The marriage knot is tied here on earth, and it includes four things:
0.9	First, both choose each other.
0.10	Second, both love each other.
0.11	Third, both are joined together.
0.12	Fourth, both are bound by promises to each other.
0.13	First, my dear sisters, both choose each other. This choosing happens in Christ as the Mediator. But it is also done by Christ as the eternal Son of God, yes, as God himself. That is true, no matter what the polished Arians and Socinians say against it.
0.14	My dear sisters, the Lord Jesus Christ chooses you only because of his free grace. He brings you into the marriage promise freely, out of his own mercy. You have offended him so badly, and yet the Lord Jesus Christ has chosen you. You did not choose him, and you would not have chosen him. But once he has chosen you, my dear sisters, then—and only then—you choose him as your Lord and Husband.
0.15	When the Lord Jesus Christ first comes to you, he finds you full of sin and filth. Because of sin, you are ugly, stained, enslaved, poor, miserable, and wretched. You are low and disgusting. He does not choose you because you are holy, or beautiful, or good enough for these things. No, the Lord Jesus Christ puts those good qualities on you, so that you are fit for his embrace. And you are drawn to choose the Lord Jesus Christ because he chose you first.
0.16	Second, my dear sisters, in this marriage of yours both love each other. This love goes along with the choosing.
0.17	Your hearts reach out for Christ. Your souls pant and long for him. You cannot rest until you are promised to this Jesus. You feel like crying out all the time, “Only Christ! Only Christ!” This is what your hearts say, if you truly feel how much you need him.
0.18	The better you get to know this Lord Jesus, the happier you are with your choice, and the more your love is drawn to him.
0.19	And where could you put your love better than on Jesus, who poured out his blood for you?
0.20	He surely deserves all your love and affection. Keep going, keep going, my dear sisters, so that your love grows stronger and stronger.
0.21	Third, there is not only choosing each other and loving each other, but also being joined together. This joining is the main thing in the marriage. Christ and souls are joined by promise, and the knot is tied so tight that no one can undo it. Not people on earth, however powerful they are. Not devils in hell, even if they put all their anger and rage together. They cannot break it. They cannot untie it. No, my dear sisters, it can never be undone. On Christ’s side, the joining is by the Spirit. On your side, it is by faith. By the Spirit, Christ takes hold of you. By faith, you take hold of him. That is how the match is made. Christ becomes yours: his person, his inheritance, and all his gifts are yours. And you become Christ’s: your selves, your hearts, and everything you have are handed over to him. Oh, may that be true more and more!
0.22	Fourth, Christ and his bride are bound by promises to each other.
0.23	Christ promises to love you here. He will not leave you. No, he will never leave you. He will protect you from the hatred of the Pharisees of our day. He will take care of you in every hard time. He will live with you here. And at last he will take you to himself, to live with him forever.
0.24	And you promise to be loving, loyal, faithful, and obedient to him. You are to stay close to him as long as you live. Then you will find that you have made the best marriage there is, for your soul and your body, for this life and for eternity.
0.25	II. Christ invites all of you to be his bride.
0.26	That is why he sends out his ministers to preach.
0.27	That is why I have come among you like this. I want you to accept this invitation. In the name of the Lord Jesus Christ I call you, and I beg you, to take him on his own terms.
0.28	He calls all of you, my sisters, who are willing to be married to the Lord Jesus Christ. Old or young, married or single, high or low—even the poorest servants. Yes, he calls you even if the world calls you “the rabble of this world.”
0.29	I tell you, the poor are just as welcome to be Christ’s bride as the rich.
0.30	He does not care more about the rich than the poor. He chose a humble young woman, engaged to a carpenter, to be his mother. And he chooses and calls people like that to be his bride. So do not be discouraged when the world looks down on you. If Christ loves you and you are married to him, that will be more than enough to make up for all the trouble you have faced here.
0.31	III. Those who want to be married to Christ must listen, think carefully, and lean toward his invitation. They must even forget their father’s house.
0.32	Those who want to be married to Christ must listen.
0.33	“Listen, O daughter.” My sisters, many of you block your ears against the calls of the gospel. You close your ears like the deaf snake that will not listen to the snake charmer, no matter how skillfully he charms.
0.34	You will not listen to Christ’s invitations. You can listen to the empty things of the world, and enjoy the world’s marriages. But you never think about Christ’s marriage, and you never enjoy it.
0.35	Sin’s temptation came in through the ear to the first man, when he turned away from God. In the same way, the invitation to be Christ’s bride must come in through the ear. Only then will the heart open to receive Jesus Christ in this marriage.
0.36	My dear sisters, if you want to be married to Christ, you must think carefully about his invitation.
0.37	Just hearing Christ’s invitation quickly or casually will not help you. It will not make the match between Christ and your souls. No, you must receive Christ in your heart. You must think about the marriage itself and its benefits. You must think about how Christ’s invitations are different from the devil’s temptations, or from anything the world offers you.
0.38	Those who want to be married to Christ must be willing to accept his invitation.
0.39	“Listen, O daughter, think it over, and bend your ear.”
0.40	This means bending your hearts. You must agree with your will. You must go along with what Christ is asking. You must long for Christ. Then your hearts will say, “Lord, let us be your bride, and you be the one we love.”
0.41	You must also forget your father’s house.
0.42	“Listen, O daughter, think it over, and forget your father’s house.”
0.43	This does not mean you must stop loving your family. But you must forget all your relatives in this way: you must be ready to give up their approval whenever it competes with the approval of the Lord Jesus Christ. And do not let your worldly friends and relatives stop you from coming to the Lord Jesus and marrying him.
0.44	I beg you with all my heart: be willing to lose anything rather than lose his favor. You must truly forget your own people. That means you must forget all the bad habits you learned in your father’s house. You must give up your empty way of living—reading plays, novels, and romances. And you must stay away from learning the songs that drunkards sing. For if you are Christ’s bride, he has bought you back.
0.45	My dear sisters, those of you who are married to the Lord Jesus Christ are very beautiful.
0.46	I do not mean your bodies. You may be less good-looking on the outside than others. But your souls will be more beautiful than anyone’s—not so much in people’s eyes as in God’s eyes. People like this have the beautiful image of God stamped on them. No one else in the world has even the smallest spark of spiritual beauty.
0.47	Those who are not married to Christ have not been made new. They are not born again. They have not been brought from sin to God. And that has to happen before you can be married to Christ.
0.48	The Lord Jesus Christ wants to see this beauty in his bride. He cries out, “My dove, you are hiding in the cracks of the rock, in the secret places of the stairway. Show me your face. Let me hear your voice. Your voice is sweet, and your face is beautiful.”
0.49	He calls his bride “my love,” because she is the one he dearly loves. He admires how lovely she is. He even says it twice in one verse: “Look how beautiful you are, my love! Look how beautiful you are!”
0.50	So you see how he describes their beauty.
0.51	And then, my sisters, Christ says something wonderful to his bride: “You have won my heart, my sister, my bride. You have won my heart with just one of your eyes, with one chain around your neck.”
0.52	So you see how pleased the Lord Jesus Christ is with his bride. So won’t you marry the Lord Jesus?
0.53	I offer Jesus Christ to all of you. Maybe you have been famous for your sins. Maybe you have been as great a prostitute as Mary Magdalene was. Once you are married to Christ, you will be forgiven.
0.54	So do not be discouraged by the insults and scorn the world may throw at you. Come and join yourselves to the Lord Jesus Christ, and all your sins will be washed away in his blood. Once you are married to Jesus, you are separated from sin. You are born again.
0.55	Right now it is as if you are married to sin. Sin is your husband, and you like it too much. But once you are married to Christ, when you are born again, then you can be said to die to sin. Until then, sin lives in the things you love. So, my sisters, give sin its deadly wound in your hearts. God’s word has called you again and again, and it has made no difference to you. But when you are married to the Lord Jesus Christ, his Spirit will bring you to him. Then you will take hold of him by faith. His Spirit will draw you to himself. He will make you willing in the day of his power. He will give you faith in him.
0.56	Faith is the hand of the soul that takes hold of Christ. So do not be satisfied until this gift of faith has been powerfully worked in you. Do not be satisfied until you have received the Lord Jesus Christ.
0.57	Hold Christ in the arms of your deepest love. You truly love the Lord Jesus Christ when you love him and value him more than your father, your mother, and all the joys and pleasures of this life. But if you enjoy anything this world can give more than the Lord Jesus Christ, you do not truly love him.
0.58	If you are married to Christ, you know him and talk with him. You will try to help his cause and make his name known in the world. When others go off to the fancy, fashionable entertainments of life, you will be working to bring honor to the Lord Jesus Christ. You will praise the one you love above every other loved one. And you will try to bring others to love him too.
0.59	My dear sisters, you have come together now to worship God. Can you show proof like this that you are married to the Lord Jesus Christ?
0.60	Oh, how joyful and comforting it is to be in this state!
0.61	Surely this marriage is worth looking for. It is the only marriage truly worth wanting. And the Lord Jesus Christ is the only lover worth looking for.
0.62	Now, my dear sisters, I will say a few words to those of you who have not yet given yourselves in marriage to the Lord Jesus.
0.63	This is a great sin. You are surely insulting the Lord who bought you.
0.64	It is also foolish of you to refuse and ignore his kind offers to make you Christ’s bride. By doing this, you throw away all the love he wants to give you. You choose rags instead of robes, trash instead of gold, pebbles instead of jewels, guilt instead of forgiveness, wounds instead of healing, dirt instead of cleansing, ugliness instead of beauty, trouble instead of peace, slavery instead of freedom, and serving the devil instead of serving Christ.
0.65	You choose shame instead of a crown, death instead of life, hell instead of heaven, and never-ending misery and torment instead of everlasting joy and glory.
0.66	Do you need any more proof of how foolish and mad you are to refuse and ignore Christ as your husband?
0.67	My dear sisters, I would take more time than you have if I tried to list every benefit you would gain by being married to the Lord Jesus.
0.68	This is your wisdom. Those who refuse are foolish virgins. But you are the wise virgins who have accepted the Lord Jesus Christ and given yourselves to him. You have made the wisest choice. The blind world may think you are fools. It may look down on you as “mad in a methodical way.” But God thinks you are wise. And one day even the people who now look down on you will see that you are wise.
0.69	It is your glory that you are married to the Lord Jesus. So be glad and proud of this marriage. But do not boast about yourselves. Boast in the Lord, who has given you these gifts so freely and kindly.
0.70	Being married to the Lord Jesus Christ keeps you safe. He will protect and defend you even from sin, from Satan, and from being lost forever. So far, then, you are safe. He cares for you when people put you in danger, and those dangerous times seem to be coming fast. Right now the danger is rising like a black cloud no bigger than a man’s hand. Soon it will spread over the whole sky, and when it is full it will burst. But if you are married to Christ, you are safe.
0.71	Now, my dear sisters, I will close with a heartfelt appeal to everyone—important and ordinary, rich and poor, all together—to be married to Christ.
0.72	Let me speak in a special way to you young women who are not yet married to Christ.
0.73	Maybe many of you came here just because you were curious. But perhaps this will be the time when you are brought home to hold on to the Lord Jesus and be married to him.
0.74	Oh, I hope that by his Spirit I can persuade you to give yourselves in marriage to the Lord of life!
0.75	If you are only brought to come to the Lord Jesus Christ and hold on to him, I will have reached my goal. Then both you and I will be glad that I preached this sermon to you.
0.76	Come, you young unmarried women, will you let me ask for your hand in marriage—not for myself, but in the name of the Lord?
0.77	Oh, I hope I can win your love and persuade you to give it to Christ!
0.78	May I be used to bring your souls to Christ!
0.79	May I be used to join you and Christ together today!
0.80	Do not be coy (bashful, shy; showing reluctance to make a definite commitment), as some of you may be in other love matters. Modesty and a young woman’s blush can look very fitting when other kinds of proposals are made to you. But here, being shy is silly, and holding back from accepting this offer is shameful. You have ten thousand times more reason to blush about refusing Christ as your beloved than about accepting him. Otherwise, the devil and sin will steal away your young hearts’ love.
0.81	No one has ever made you a better offer. No one has ever offered you a match like this one: to be joined in marriage to the Lord Jesus Christ.
0.82	Think about who the Lord Jesus is, the one you are invited to marry. He is the best husband. No one can compare with Jesus Christ.
0.83	Do you want someone who is great?
0.84	He has the highest honor there is. He is the glory of heaven, the darling of eternity. Angels admire him, devils fear him, and saints worship him.
0.85	Think of being married to such a great king! How much honor will that marriage bring you?
0.86	Do you want someone who is rich?
0.87	No one compares with Christ. Everything that fills the earth belongs to him.
0.88	If you are married to Christ, you will share in his riches, which no one can measure. Here you will receive from his fullness, one gift of grace after another. And later you will be welcomed into glory, and you will live with this Jesus forever and ever.
0.89	Do you want someone who is wise?
0.90	No one compares with Christ for wisdom.
0.91	His knowledge has no limit, and his wisdom matches it.
0.92	If you are married to Christ, he will guide you and give you advice. He will make you wise enough to be saved.
0.93	Do you want someone who is powerful? Someone who can protect you from your enemies, and from all the insults and put-downs of the Pharisees of our day?
0.94	No one is as powerful as Christ. The Lord Jesus Christ has all power.
0.95	Do you want someone who is good?
0.96	No one is like Christ in this. Others may have some goodness, but it is not perfect. Christ’s goodness is complete and perfect. He is full of goodness, and there is no evil in him at all.
0.97	Do you want someone who is beautiful?
0.98	His eyes sparkle more than any others. His loving looks and glances take your breath away. His smiles are the most delightful and refreshing thing for the soul. Christ is the loveliest person in the whole world.
0.99	Do you want someone who can love you?
0.100	No one can love you like Christ. My dear sisters, his love is too great to understand. His love is greater than every other love. The love of the Lord Jesus came first; it has no beginning. His love is free; nothing earned it. His love is huge; it cannot be measured. His love is steady; it never changes. And his love lasts forever.
0.101	My dear sisters, it was the love of the Lord Jesus Christ that brought him down from heaven. Love covered his divine nature with a human soul and body—for he is God over all, blessed forever. Love made him suffer hunger, thirst, and sadness. He was humbled all the way to death for you. For you who are married to him, he went through the painful, shameful, and disgraceful death of the cross. My sisters, can you hear this and not be moved when you think that the blessed Jesus went through all of this for sinful creatures like you and me?
0.102	Out of love he finished the work of rescuing us on earth, everything needed to make full payment for sin. Then his love carried him back to heaven, where he had been before. There he could apply to his people what he had bought for them. There he could pray for those he had rescued. And there he could prepare a place for them—glorious homes with himself, in the house not made with hands, which lasts forever in heaven.
0.103	It is out of love that he sends gifts like these from heaven to his people on earth. He brings them to his people by his Spirit, through the acts of worship he has commanded.
0.104	So surely no one deserves as much as the Lord Jesus Christ to be the one you marry. If you are married to Christ, he is yours—all that he is and all that he has. You will have his heart, and you will share in the best signs of his dearest love.
0.105	My dear sisters, the Lord Jesus Christ is begging you to be his bride.
0.106	We ministers have been sent by the Lord Jesus Christ to invite you to this very thing in his name. And Christ’s invitations are real, open to all, frequent, earnest, and free.
0.107	Christ’s invitation for you to be his bride is real. The marriage is real, and so, my dear sisters, you are really invited to it.
0.108	The Lord is not mocking you or pretending with you. Some fake lovers do that. They pretend to love young women until they win their hearts. Then they dishonestly and cruelly drop them, because they never really meant to get engaged or married. But the Lord really means it when he invites you. He has never thrown away anyone whose agreement and love he had won. Next,
0.109	My dear sisters, Christ’s invitations to you are open to all.
0.110	All of you are invited. None of you are left out. All kinds of sinners are invited. The worst and most disgusting sinners, the most famous lawbreakers, are invited to be Christ’s bride. They will be just as welcome as anyone else in the arms of his love.
0.111	Christ’s invitations to you are frequent. Jesus Christ calls you often. He has waited for you again and again, year after year. And today he is inviting you, through me, to come to him.
0.112	Do not brush off this invitation. Receive it with joy and thankfulness.
0.113	I beg you, come to this Jesus, who is inviting you like this to be his bride. Next,
0.114	Christ’s invitations to be his bride are earnest. He calls you—and not only calls, but calls with all his heart. Yes, he gives you many reasons. He will urge you to come to him. He does not want to take no for an answer. He knocks, and knocks hard, at the door of your hearts, asking to be let in. And surely you will not turn away the Lord of life and glory, who died for you and gave himself for you. Oh, my dear sisters, let this be the evening you are engaged to the Lord Jesus Christ!
0.115	He invites you to be his bride for free, because all his invitations are free. He does not expect you to bring money or property with you, as worldly lovers do. He wants nothing from you. In fact, you must come with nothing if you are to be married to the Lord Jesus Christ.
0.116	If you are poor, miserable, blind, and naked, Jesus Christ will make up for all of it out of his own free mercy. He will fill you and give you everything from his treasure house. He will make you fit for himself. He will prepare you to live with him forever.
0.117	Think about this: once you are married to Christ, once you are joined to him like this, you will never be separated from him. Neither people nor devils will be able to pull you apart. No one, no one, will separate you. Death breaks every other bond, but it will not break the marriage bond between you and Christ. Instead, death will bring you to have the one you love completely and forever.
0.118	So what do you say now, young women?
0.119	Will I get a “yes” to take back to my master? Or will I be sent away with a “no”? No, I cannot take a message like that to my master. I hope for better things from you, my sisters—things that go along with being saved. It seems to me that by now you should be starting to want Jesus Christ. You look as if you want him. You listen as if you are ready to say yes.
0.120	What do you say?
0.121	Will the match between Christ and your souls be made this evening?
0.122	Oh, I wish I could be the one who joins your hands together—or rather, your hearts! Oh, I wish I could be the one who ties the knot that can never be untied!
0.123	Some people marry in a hurry and are sorry for a long time afterward. But if you were once married to Jesus Christ, you would never be sorry. The only thing that would make you sad is that you did not join him sooner. And you would not be separated from him again for the whole world.
0.124	Will this be the day you are engaged to him?
0.125	Some of you have waited a long time. Will you wait any longer?
0.126	If you will not come now, you may never get another chance. This may be the last time you are asked, so it is dangerous to say no. Some of you are very young, too young for other engagements. But, my dear sisters, none of you are too young to be married to the Lord Jesus Christ. For other engagements you need your parents’ permission. But for this one, the decision is yours. You may give yourselves to Christ, and you should, whether your parents agree or not.
0.127	But some of you may ask, “What must we do to be married to Christ?”
0.128	You must feel your need to be married to him. Until you feel your need for the Lord Jesus Christ, you cannot be married to him. You must long for this Jesus and seek him, asking for a share in him. You must cry out to him every night to take you as his bride. Take off the filth of sin and everything that makes you dirty. And then, my sisters, put on the white clothes, the clean garments, that Christ has provided for you: the robes of his righteousness. In these clothes you will be beautiful. In these clothes you will be accepted. You must be wearing the wedding garment. You must take off all your own good works, because they will only keep you away from Christ. No, you must come without any goodness of your own that comes from keeping the law. You must have the righteousness of Christ.
0.129	So come to the Lord Jesus Christ, and he will give it to you. He will not send you away without it.
0.130	Take him on his own terms, and he is yours forever. Oh, give yourselves to him—soul, body, and everything—to be his forever. Then Christ will be yours. And then how happy, how happy you will be that you were ever born!
0.131	But if any of you die before you are married to the Lord Jesus Christ, then how terrible, how terrible for you that you were ever alive! But if you go to Christ, you will be married to the Lord Jesus. No matter how great your sins have been, the blood of Christ will make you clean from them. The marriage promise between Christ and your souls will wipe away all your sins. Then you will be tired of your old ways, because everything in your souls will become new.
0.132	Now, my dear sisters, I will finish by saying just a word or two to those of you who are already married to the Lord Jesus Christ.
0.133	Oh, be amazed, be amazed at the rich and free grace that has brought you into this relationship! Isn’t this the greatest example of love—that you should be the bride of the Lord Jesus Christ?
0.134	You had no beauty. You had no loveliness. You were full of sin. And yet he would embrace people like you and me! We would be taken into the arms of this Lord Jesus!
0.135	Oh, what endless kindness, stooping down so low!
0.136	Oh, what amazing love! I beg you, honor, honor this Lord Jesus Christ with deep respect.
0.137	He is your Lord. You must honor him, love him, and be faithful to him. Obey him, and be careful to please him in everything. Try to stay close to him in fellowship every day. Watch for Christ’s second coming, long for it, and get ready for it. Then the wedding between you will be celebrated, and you will live with him in homes of never-ending joy. There you will love and live with this King of glory forever and ever.
0.138	I do not know how to stop. I feel I could speak to you until midnight, if it would bring you to the Lord Jesus Christ and make you his bride. Truly, that marriage will bring you the greatest benefit, as you will find out for yourselves if you will only try it. And to help you do that, I will keep praying and keep working.
0.139	My dear sisters, I will spend everything I have, and spend myself too, for you. With God’s help I will keep going in this work I have started. Many of you may have time some evening during the week without taking time away from your work. So, my sisters, I will be either here or at a place that will be publicly announced. I will not care if people insult me or look down on me. The people of this world can call me whatever they like. They can say I am a Methodist.
0.140	In fact, my sisters, by God’s grace I have decided to use every method I can to pull you away from Satan, so that you may be like burning sticks snatched out of the fire. This will be my method. I hope it will be the way your precious souls, which will live forever, are truly saved.
0.141	If I am the one used to do this, I will be glad. Yes, I will be glad, no matter what people or devils say or do against it. May the Lord Jesus Christ guide me and help me at all times to do what brings him the most glory and what is best for your souls. And may you all say a heartfelt “Amen” to that.
0.142	“Now may the Lord Jesus Christ, who is God over all, blessed forever, help you and watch over you. May he keep you from all evil and sin here. And may he bring you before his Father without any fault on the great day when all must give account!
0.143	To this Lord Jesus Christ, the Father, and the blessed Spirit—three persons, yet only one eternal and invisible God—be all honor, power, glory, might, majesty, and rule, now, from now on, and forever. Amen, Amen.”
0.144	“May the grace of our Lord Jesus Christ, the love of God, and the fellowship of the Holy Spirit be with you all. May they comfort you in trouble and rescue you from it. May they keep you safe as you go to your homes. And when you are there, may they keep you in awe of him, so that you live for his glory. Then to live will be Christ, and to die will be your eternal gain. And so may you live with him through all the ages and sing Hallelujahs to him forever. Amen.”
