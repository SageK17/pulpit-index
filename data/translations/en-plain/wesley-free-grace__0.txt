0.195	But what decree? This one: “I will set before the human race ‘life and death, blessing and curse.’
0.196	And the soul who chooses life will live, just as the soul who chooses death will die.”
0.197	This decree, by which “those God knew beforehand, he predestined,” truly was made from everlasting. By this decree, all who allow Christ to make them alive are “chosen according to what God knew beforehand.” It now stands firm, like the moon and like the faithful witnesses in heaven. And when heaven and earth pass away, this will not pass away. For it is as unchangeable and eternal as God himself, who made it.
0.198	This decree gives us the strongest encouragement to be full of good works and all holiness. It is also a spring of joy and happiness, bringing us great comfort that never ends.
0.199	This is worthy of God. It fits in every way with all the perfect qualities of his nature.
0.200	It gives us the noblest picture of his justice, mercy, and truth.
0.201	The whole message of the Christian revelation agrees with this, and so do all its parts.
0.202	Moses and all the Prophets bear witness to this, and so do our blessed Lord and all his Apostles. So Moses says, in the name of his Lord: “I call heaven and earth to testify against you this day: I have put life and death in front of you, blessing and cursing. So choose life, so that you and your offspring may live.”
0.203	So Ezekiel says (to quote one prophet for them all): “The soul that sins will die. The son will not bear” forever “the wrongdoing of the father.
