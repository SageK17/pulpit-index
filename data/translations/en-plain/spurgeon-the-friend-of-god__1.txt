0.300	IV. I will be finished once I have said a few words about the last point, which is THE TITLE PUT TO USE in practical ways.
0.301	These are the practical uses.
0.302	First, this is a great encouragement for God’s people.
0.303	Look at what is possible for you.
0.304	Not long ago, on a Sunday morning, I tried to say something about what saints may become in the future. After all, the servant who was faithful with his pound was put in charge of ten cities.
0.305	It has not yet been shown what we will be. We do not have even the beginning of an idea of what we will be in the next world if we are found faithful to our Master here. Nor do we know the glory that will transform us on the day Christ comes, and during the thousand years of his glorious reign on the earth.
0.306	But now I want you to notice the prize of your high calling in this life.
0.307	You can become God’s friends. You can be so clearly on his side that people will call you the friends of God.
0.308	How few people reach this!
0.309	Do you know even one person like that?
0.310	Think of all the Christians you know, and tell me how many of them could be called friends of God.
0.311	I know one man like that, but I will not say his name.
0.312	I am afraid he may not be on earth much longer, because he is very old.
0.313	He is a man who has trusted God, walked with God, and been faithful to God. Because of this, God has greatly honored him by letting him carry on a huge and very useful work.
0.314	I wish I could grow to be like him, but next to him I feel like a little baby.
0.315	He is a rare man.
0.316	Why aren’t there more people like him?
0.317	Is it because God’s arm is too short?
0.318	No. It is because our sins hide him from us.
0.319	We could be—and we should be—such men and women that the people who know us at home and at work would see that we are friends of Jesus.
0.320	As a preacher, I would like people to say this about me: that I upheld the glory of my Lord, defended the teachings of his cross, and was a friend of the old gospel while others were chasing after new ideas.
0.321	In one way or another, we should aim for this heavenly friendship.
0.322	See that this is within your reach—and make it real right away.
0.323	Next, here is a serious thought for those who want to be God’s friends.
0.324	A friend must act like a friend and take tender care of his friend.
0.325	One small word from a friend will hurt you much more than a harsh lie from an enemy.
0.326	Remember how the Savior said, “It was not an enemy—I could have put up with that. But it was you, a man like me, someone I knew well.”
0.327	“The Lord your God is a jealous God.” If he brings any of us so close to him that we become his friends, then his jealousy burns like coals of juniper wood, with a very fierce flame.
0.328	My brother, God will save you in spite of a thousand faults. But he will not call you his friend unless you take great care to please him in everything.
0.329	Should we back away from this honor because of the responsibility?
0.330	No, we are glad to have the responsibility. We long to please God.
0.331	Our God is a consuming fire, but we still long to live in him.
0.332	Our new nature feels at home in this fire.
0.333	Even now we pray that this fire will purify us and burn away all the dross and tin in us.
0.334	We would gladly be baptized with fire.
0.335	We do not want anything to be spared that should be burned up or that can be burned up.
0.336	We accept friendship with God on his terms.
0.337	I tremble as I say this.
0.338	We are willing to go through anything that will make us one with God.
0.339	God’s Spirit is the Spirit of judgment and the Spirit of burning. Do you know what you are asking for when you pray to be filled with him?
0.340	I hope you will answer, “Whatever it may be, I want to feel that heavenly power that can make me God’s friend forever.”
