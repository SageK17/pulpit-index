0.213	1 Father of everyone, your powerful voice / Called this whole universe into being. / Your mercies rejoice over all, / And they stay the same through endless ages. / You hold everything together by your word. / You show your generous love to all. / You hear the call of every creature you made, / And you fill every mouth with good things.
0.214	2 You rule in heaven, sitting on a throne of light. / All of nature is spread out beneath you. / Earth, air, and sea lie open before your eyes. / So does the deep darkness of hell. / Wisdom, strength, and love belong to you. / We bow down low before you. / We confess your divine qualities. / We praise you as the Sovereign Lord of all.
0.215	3 Sovereign Lord, let everything that moves / On the earth, in the air, or in the sky confess you. / Let them honor your power and praise your goodness. / Let them tremble before your eye that sees everything. / All of you who owe your life to Him, / Spend every hour of your day praising Him! / Jehovah reigns! Be glad, O earth! / Shout for joy, you morning stars!
0.216	4 Son, loved by your Father forever, / Take up your mighty power. / Let everyone on earth experience your mercy. / Let them all worship the grace you showed when you bled. / Show everyone how your love wins. / Be the only King in every heart. / Keep on until all your enemies admit that you rule. / Then glory will finish what grace began.
0.217	5 Spirit of grace, health, and power, / You are a fountain of light and love here below. / Pour out your healing power everywhere. / Let it flow over all the nations. / Set our hearts on fire with perfect love. / Finish the work of faith in us. / Then the armies of heaven will not move any faster / Than we on earth move to do your will.
0.218	6 Father, each day it is your work to give / Your children a fresh supply of what they need. / You dress the lilies in the field. / You hear the young ravens when they cry. / We throw all our worries on you. We live / Because of you, and you know everything we need. / Oh, feed us with your grace! Give / Our souls the bread of life today!
0.219	7 Eternal Lamb of God, without any stain, / You were killed before the world was made. / Sprinkle us with your blood always. / Oh, wash us clean and keep us clean forever. / Toward every person (all praise to you!) / Stir our hearts with deep compassion. / That way all people will be able to see / That God is in us, because God is love.
0.220	8 You give life, and you are the Lord of life. Your power / And your protecting care are free for everyone. / When temptation hits us hard, / Let us run to you, away from sin and Satan. / Lord, we belong to you, and you belong to us. / Show all your goodness in us. / Make our hearts new, make them bigger, and fill them / With peace and joy, with heaven, and with God.
0.221	9 Blessing and honor, praise and love / You Three, who are equal and eternal together: / On earth below and in heaven above, / Let all your works give them to you. / Three times Holy! The kingdom is yours. / All power belongs to you. / And when everything that was created dies, / Your glory will keep on shining forever.
