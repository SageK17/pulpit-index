0.1	1. We just heard the holy Gospel being read. It has given us a warning about the forgiveness of sins.
0.2	Now my sermon must give you a warning about this same subject.
0.3	We are servants of the word. It is not our own word, but the word of our God and Lord. No one serves Him without receiving glory, and no one despises Him without being punished.
0.4	He is the Lord our God. While He remained with the Father, He made us. Then He Himself was made for our sake, and He made us new. This Lord our God, Jesus Christ Himself, tells us what we have just heard in the Gospel.
0.5	“If your brother sins against you,” He says, “rebuke him. If he repents, forgive him. And if he sins against you seven times in a day, and comes and says, I repent, forgive him.”
0.6	He does not want “seven times in a day” to mean anything other than “as often as it happens.” Otherwise your brother might sin eight times, and you would refuse to forgive him.
0.7	So what does “seven times” mean?
0.8	It means always, as often as he sins and repents.
0.9	One psalm says, “Seven times a day I will praise You.” That means the same thing as another psalm that says, “His praise will always be in my mouth.”
0.10	There is a very strong reason why seven times stands for always. All of time moves around in a circle of seven days that come and then come back again.
0.11	2. So whoever you are, if your thoughts are set on Christ and you want to receive what He has promised, do not be slow to do what He has commanded.
0.12	What has He promised?
0.13	“Eternal life.” And what has He commanded?
0.14	That you forgive your brother.
0.15	It is as if He said to you, “You are a human being. Forgive another human being, so that I, who am God, may come to you.”
0.16	For now I will pass over, or rather pass by for a while, God’s even greater promises. In them our Creator promises to make us equal to His angels, so that we may live forever with Him, in Him, and through Him. Leaving that aside for now, don’t you want to receive from your God the very thing you are commanded to give your brother?
0.17	I say it again: this very thing you are commanded to give your brother, don’t you want to receive it from your Lord?
0.18	Tell me if you don’t want it. Then don’t give it.
0.19	What does this mean? It means you should forgive the person who asks you, if you need to be forgiven yourself.
0.20	But if you have nothing that needs to be forgiven, then, I dare to say, refuse to forgive.
0.21	But I should not even say this.
0.22	Even if you have nothing that needs to be forgiven, forgive.
0.23	3. You are about to say to me, “But I am not God. I am a human being, a sinner.”
0.24	Thank God that you admit you have sins.
0.25	So forgive, so that your sins may be forgiven.
0.26	Yet the Lord our God Himself urges us to imitate Him.
0.27	First of all, God Himself, Christ, urges us. The Apostle Peter said about Him, “Christ suffered for us. He left you an example, so that you would follow in His steps. He did no sin, and no deceit was found in His mouth.”
0.28	He truly had no sin. Yet He died for our sins and shed His blood so that sins could be forgiven.
0.29	For our sake He took on Himself what He did not deserve, so that He could set us free from what we did deserve.
0.30	He did not deserve death, and we did not deserve life.
0.31	Why? Because we were sinners.
0.32	He did not deserve death, and we did not deserve life. He received what He did not deserve. He gave what we did not deserve.
0.33	But we are speaking about the forgiveness of sins. So that you do not think it is too high a thing to imitate Christ, listen to the Apostle. He says, “Forgive one another, just as God in Christ has forgiven you.”
0.34	“So imitate God.”
0.35	These are the Apostle’s words, not mine.
0.36	Is it really proud to imitate God?
0.37	Listen to the Apostle: “Imitate God, as dearly loved children.”
0.38	You are called a child. If you refuse to imitate Him, why do you want His inheritance?
0.39	4. I would say this even if you had no sin that you wanted to have forgiven.
0.40	But as things are, whoever you are, you are a human being. Even if you are righteous, you are a human being. Whether you are a layperson, a monk, a member of the clergy, a bishop, or an apostle, you are a human being.
0.41	Listen to what the Apostle says: “If we say that we have no sin, we deceive ourselves.”
0.42	This is the famous John, who was also an Evangelist. The Lord Christ loved him more than all the others, and he lay against His chest. He is the one who says, “If we say.”
0.43	He did not say, “If you say that you have no sin.” He said, “If we say that we have no sin, we deceive ourselves, and the truth is not in us.”
0.44	He included himself in the guilt, so that he would also be included in the pardon.
0.45	“If we say.”
0.46	Think about who is saying this: “If we say that we have no sin, we deceive ourselves, and the truth is not in us.
0.47	But if we confess our sins, He is faithful and just to forgive us our sins and to cleanse us from all wickedness.”
0.48	How does He cleanse us?
0.49	By forgiving. He does this not because He finds nothing to punish, but because He finds something to forgive.
0.50	So, brothers, if we have sins, let us forgive those who ask us.
0.51	Let us not hold on to hostility in our hearts against anyone.
0.52	Holding on to hostility corrupts our hearts more than anything else.
0.53	5. So I want you to forgive, because I find you asking for forgiveness.
0.54	You are being asked, so forgive. You are being asked, and you yourself will ask. You are being asked, so forgive. You will ask to be forgiven. For look, the time of prayer will come. I will hold you to the words you will have to say.
0.55	You will say, “Our Father, who is in heaven.”
0.56	For you will not be one of the children if you do not say, “Our Father.”
0.57	So you will say, “Our Father, who is in heaven.”
0.58	Go on: “May Your name be held holy.”
0.59	Keep going: “May Your kingdom come.”
0.60	Go on further: “May Your will be done, as in heaven, so on earth.”
0.61	Look at what you add next: “Give us today our daily bread.”
0.62	Where are your riches?
0.63	So you are a beggar.
0.64	But for now, since this is the point I am talking about, say what comes after “Give us today our daily bread.”
0.65	Say what follows: “Forgive us our debts.”
0.66	Now you have come to the words I am talking about: “Forgive us our debts.”
0.67	By what right? By what agreement? On what condition? On what clear terms?
0.68	“As we also forgive those who owe us.”
0.69	Not forgiving is only a small part of it. You do even more: you lie to God.
0.70	The condition is set. The law is fixed.
0.71	“Forgive as I forgive.”
0.72	So He does not forgive unless you forgive.
0.73	“Forgive as I forgive.”
0.74	You want to be forgiven when you ask. So forgive the person who asks you.
0.75	The One who is an expert in heaven’s laws gave us the words of these prayers. He does not trick you. Ask in the exact words of His heavenly voice. Say, “Forgive us, as we also forgive,” and do what you say.
0.76	Whoever lies in his prayers loses the benefit he is asking for. Whoever lies in his prayers loses his case and also receives his punishment.
0.77	If someone lies to the emperor, he is proven guilty of his lie when he comes. But when you lie in prayer, your own prayer proves you guilty.
0.78	For God does not look for a witness against you to prove you guilty.
0.79	The One who gave you the words of the prayers is your Advocate. If you lie, He is a witness against you. If you do not change your ways, He will be your Judge.
0.80	So say it, and do it too.
0.81	If you do not say it, you will not get what you ask for, because you are asking against the law. But if you say it and do not do it, you will also be guilty of lying.
0.82	There is no way to escape that line except by doing what we say.
0.83	Can we erase this line from our prayer?
0.84	Do you want the words “Forgive us our debts” to stay, and us to erase what follows, “As we also forgive those who owe us”?
0.85	You must not erase it, or you yourself will be erased first.
0.86	So in this prayer you say, “Give,” and you say, “Forgive.” You say this so that you may receive what you do not have, and be forgiven for the wrong you have done.
0.87	So if you want to receive, give. If you want to be forgiven, forgive.
0.88	That is a short summary.
0.89	Listen to Christ Himself in another place: “Forgive, and you will be forgiven.”
0.90	What will you forgive?
0.91	The sins others have committed against you.
0.92	What will you be forgiven?
0.93	The sins you yourselves have committed.
0.94	“Forgive.” “Give, and what you desire will be given to you”: eternal life.
0.95	Support the poor man’s life in this world. Sustain the poor man’s present life. And for such a small, earthly seed you will receive a harvest of eternal life. Amen.
