3.135	My friend, why not call on God right now, as David did when he came to his senses? Pray the same prayer he prayed. How thankful we should be that we have that prayer! Why not pray it on your knees right now?

H:3.62	Examples from History
3.153	But you say you don’t believe the Bible.
3.154	Then look at history, and see whether this law is true.
3.155	Maxentine built a fake bridge to drown Constantine, but he was the one who drowned.

3.158	Valens had about eighty Christians put on a ship, sent out to sea, and burned alive. Later he was beaten by the Goths and ran away to a cottage, where he was burned alive.
3.159	Alexander VI was poisoned by wine he had prepared for someone else.
3.160	Henry III of France was stabbed in the same room where he had helped plan the cruel massacre of French Protestants.
3.161	Marie Antoinette was riding to Notre Dame Cathedral for her wedding. She told the soldiers to order all the beggars, the crippled, and the people in rags to get away from the route of the parade.
3.162	She could not stand to look at these poor, miserable people.
3.163	Not long after, she was tied up in the executioner’s cart, riding to the place where she would be put to death. Crowds stared at her with hearts as cold as ice and as hard as granite.
3.164	When Foulon was asked how the starving people were supposed to live, he said, “Let them eat grass.”
3.165	Later, a furious mob caught him in the streets of Paris. They hanged him, stuck his head on a pike, and stuffed his mouth with grass.
T:4	You Reap More Than You Sow
4.1	“But some seed landed on good ground and gave a harvest—some a hundred times what was sown, some sixty times, some thirty times.”—Matthew 13:8.
4.2	If I sow one bushel of seed, I expect to reap ten or twenty bushels.
4.3	In one day I can sow what it will take ten men to reap.
4.4	The Spanish have a saying: “Sow a thought, and you reap an act.
4.5	Sow an act, and you reap a habit.
4.6	Sow a habit, and you reap a character.
4.7	Sow a character, and you reap a destiny.”
4.8	And reaping takes longer than sowing.
4.9	I have heard of a kind of bean that multiplies itself a thousand times.
4.10	People say that a single bit of thistle fluff blew off the deck of a ship and covered a whole South Sea island with thistles.
4.11	The oak grows from an acorn. The mighty Mississippi River starts from a little spring.
4.12	One glass of whisky can lead to dying as a drunkard.
4.13	One lie can ruin a person’s career.
4.14	One mistake made when you are young can follow you all your life.
4.15	Someone has said that many Christians spend half their time trying to keep down the sprouts of seeds they sowed when they were young.
4.16	If it is not kept under control, the wish to “have a drink” will grow into a thirst that eats you up. The wish to “play a game of cards” will grow into a gambler’s passion you cannot resist.
