10.198	7. “For this is the love of God: that we keep His commandments.”
10.199	You have already heard, “All the law and the prophets hang on these two commandments.”
10.200	See how He doesn’t want you to spread yourself thin over lots and lots of pages: “All the law and the prophets hang on these two commandments.”
10.201	Which two commandments?
10.202	“Love the Lord your God with all your heart, with all your soul, and with all your mind.
10.203	And love your neighbor as yourself.
10.204	All the law and the prophets hang on these two commandments.”
10.205	So you see which commandments this whole letter is talking about.
10.206	So hold on to love, and let your minds be at rest.
10.207	Why are you afraid that you might do harm to someone?
10.208	Who harms a person he loves?
10.209	Just love. It is impossible to love without doing good.
10.210	But maybe you correct someone?
10.211	Kindness does that, not harshness.
10.212	But maybe you give him a beating?
10.213	You do it to train him. Your loving kindness won’t let you leave him without discipline.
10.214	And in fact, somehow the results turn out different, even opposite. Sometimes hatred acts charming, and love acts fierce.
10.215	Someone hates his enemy but pretends to be his friend. He sees him doing something wrong, and he praises him. He wants him to rush headlong. He wants him to go blindly over the cliff of his cravings and maybe never come back. So he praises him, for “the sinner is praised in the desires of his soul.” He rubs him with the oil of flattery. See? He hates him, and he praises him.
10.216	Someone else sees his friend doing the same kind of thing. He calls him back. If his friend won’t listen, he even uses harsh words. He scolds. He argues. Sometimes it comes to this: you even have to argue!
10.217	See? Hatred acts sweet and gentle, and love argues!
10.218	Don’t just look at words that seem kind, or at a correction that seems harsh. Look at the source they come from. Look for the root they grow from.
10.219	One person is gentle and smooth so that he can deceive. The other argues so that he can correct.
10.220	So, brothers and sisters, we can’t make your hearts bigger. Ask God for the gift of loving one another.
10.221	Love everyone, even your enemies. Don’t love them because they are your brothers. Love them so that they may become your brothers. Then you will always burn with love for your brothers. You will love the one who has already become your brother. And you will love your enemy, so that by being loved he may become your brother.
10.222	Whenever you love a brother, you love a friend.
10.223	He is already with you. He is already joined to you in unity, yes, in catholic unity.
10.224	If you are living rightly, you love a brother who was once an enemy.
10.225	But suppose you love someone who doesn’t believe in Christ yet. Or maybe he believes, but only the way demons believe. You scold him for his emptiness.
10.226	Keep loving him, and love him like a brother. He isn’t a brother yet. But you love him so that he may become one.
10.227	So all our love is love for our brothers. It is for Christians, for all His members.
10.228	My brothers and sisters, think of the training that love gives. Think of its strength, its flowers, its fruit, its beauty, and its sweetness. Think of its food, its drink, its nourishment, and its embrace. You can never get too much of it.
10.229	If it gives us so much joy while we are far from home, how glad we will be when we reach our own homeland!
10.230	8. So let’s run, my brothers and sisters. Let’s run and love Christ.
10.231	Which Christ? Jesus Christ.
10.232	Who is He? The Word of God.
10.233	And how did He come to the sick?
10.234	“The Word became flesh and lived in us.”
10.235	So what Scripture predicted has come true: “Christ had to suffer and rise again from the dead on the third day.”
10.236	Where is His body?
10.237	Where are His members working hard?
10.238	Where do you need to be so that you are under your Head?
10.239	“And that repentance and forgiveness of sins should be preached in His name through all nations, starting at Jerusalem.”
10.240	That is where your love should spread.
10.241	Christ says it, and the Psalm says it too, that is, the Spirit of God: “Your commandment is extremely broad.” And yet someone wants love to be shut up in Africa!
10.242	If you want to love Christ, spread your love over the whole earth. Christ’s members are all over the earth.
10.243	If you love only a part, you are divided. If you are divided, you are not in the body. If you are not in the body, you are not under the Head.
10.244	What good does it do you to believe and also to insult Him?
10.245	You worship Him in the Head, but you insult Him in the Body.
10.246	He loves His Body.
10.247	You may have cut yourself off from His Body. But the Head has not cut itself off from its Body.
10.248	Your Head calls out to you from above: “It is useless for you to honor Me. It is useless for you to honor Me.”
10.249	It is just as if a man wanted to kiss your head while stepping on your feet. Maybe he is wearing boots with nails, and he crushes your feet while he holds your head and kisses it. Wouldn’t you cry out, right in the middle of his kind words? Wouldn’t you say, “What are you doing, man? You are stepping on me!”
10.250	You wouldn’t say, “You are stepping on my head.” After all, he honored your head. But the head would cry out more for the parts being stepped on than for itself because it was honored.
10.251	Doesn’t the head itself cry out, “I don’t want your honor. Stop stepping on me”?
10.252	Now say it, if you can: “How have I stepped on you?” Say this to the head: “I wanted to kiss you. I wanted to hug you.”
10.253	But you fool, don’t you see? What you want to hug is joined to what you are stepping on. There is a unity that holds the whole body together.
10.254	Up above you honor me, but down below you step on me.
10.255	The part you step on hurts more than the part you honor is glad.
10.256	How does the tongue cry out?
10.257	It says, “It hurts me.” It doesn’t say, “It hurts my foot.” It says, “It hurts me.”
10.258	Tongue, who touched you? Who hit you? Who poked you? Who pricked you?
10.259	“No one. But I am joined to the parts that are being stepped on.”
10.260	“How could I not feel pain, when I am not separate from them?”
10.261	9. So our Lord Jesus Christ went up to heaven on the fortieth day. As He went, He entrusted His Body to us, where it would go on lying. He had a reason for this. He saw that many would honor Him because He had gone up to heaven. And He saw that their honor is useless if they trample on His members here on earth.
10.262	Someone might go wrong and worship the Head in heaven while trampling on the feet on earth. So that no one would do that, He told us where His members would be.
10.263	When He was about to go up, He spoke His last words on earth. After those words He never spoke on earth again.
10.264	The Head, about to go up to heaven, entrusted His members on earth to us, and then He left.
10.265	From then on, you don’t find Christ speaking on earth. You find Him speaking, but from heaven.
10.266	And why did He speak even from heaven?
10.267	Because His members on earth were being trampled on.
10.268	He spoke from above to Saul, the persecutor: “Saul, Saul, why are you persecuting Me?”
10.269	“I have gone up to heaven, but I still lie on earth. Here I sit at the Father’s right hand. But there I am still hungry and thirsty, and I am a stranger.”
10.270	So how did He entrust His Body to us when He was about to go up to heaven?
10.271	The disciples asked Him, “Lord, will You show Yourself now? And when will the kingdom of Israel come?”
10.272	He was just about to leave, and He answered, “It is not for you to know the time the Father has kept in His own power. But you will receive strength when the Holy Spirit comes on you, and you will be My witnesses.”
10.273	See where His Body is spread out. See where He does not want to be trampled on: “You will be My witnesses to Jerusalem, and to Judea, and even to the whole earth.”
10.274	“Look where I lie, I who am going up!
10.275	I go up because I am the Head. My Body is still lying below.”
10.276	Where is it lying? All over the earth.
10.277	“Don’t strike it. Don’t hurt it. Don’t trample on it.” These are Christ’s last words as He was about to go into heaven.
10.278	Think of a sick man wasting away in bed at home. He is worn out by his illness and close to death. His soul is almost ready to leave his lips. Maybe he is worried about something dear to him, something he loves very much. It comes to his mind, and he calls his heirs. He says to them, “Please, do this.”
10.279	In a way, he holds his soul back by force, so that it won’t leave before those words are settled.
10.280	He speaks those last words, then he breathes his last, and his body is carried to the grave.
10.281	How do his heirs remember the last words of that dying man?
10.282	Suppose someone stood up and told them, “Don’t do it.” What would they say?
10.283	“What? Shouldn’t I do what my father told me with his very last breath? Those were the last words I heard from him as he was leaving this life.
10.284	I may ignore other things he said, but his last words have a stronger hold on me. After that I never saw him again. I never heard him speak again.”
10.285	Brothers and sisters, think about this with Christian hearts. A man’s words, spoken as he was about to go to the grave, are so sweet, so precious, and so important to his heirs. Then how should we value the last words of Christ? He spoke them not as He was about to go back to the grave, but as He was about to go up to heaven!
10.286	When a man has lived and died, his soul is taken off to other places, and his body is buried in the ground. It makes no difference to him whether his words are carried out or not. He now has something else to do, or something else to suffer. Either he is rejoicing in Abraham’s bosom, or he is in eternal fire, longing for a drop of water. Meanwhile, his body lies in the grave without feeling anything. And yet people still keep the last words of the dying man.
10.287	So what can people expect if they don’t keep the last words of the One who sits in heaven? He sees from above whether His words are despised or not.
10.288	These are the words of the One who said, “Saul, Saul, why are you persecuting Me?” He keeps a record for the judgment of everything He sees His members suffer.
10.289	10. “But what have we done?” they say.
10.290	“We are the ones being persecuted, not the persecutors.”
10.291	You are the persecutors, you miserable people.
10.292	First of all, you have divided the Church.
10.293	The sword of the tongue is stronger than a sword of steel.
10.294	Hagar, Sarah’s servant, was proud. Her mistress treated her harshly because of her pride.
10.295	That was discipline, not punishment.
10.296	So when Hagar ran away from her mistress, what did the angel say to her?
10.297	“Go back to your mistress.”
10.298	So, you worldly soul, you are like a proud slave woman. Suppose you have suffered some trouble to discipline you. Why are you raging?
10.299	“Go back to your mistress.” Hold on to the peace of the Church.
10.300	Look, we bring out the Gospels. We read where the Church is spread out. People argue against us and call us “Betrayers!”
10.301	Betrayers of what? Christ entrusts His Church to us, and you don’t believe it. So should I believe you when you insult my parents?
10.302	Do you want me to believe what you say about “betrayers”?
10.303	First, believe Christ yourself.
10.304	What is worth believing?
10.305	Christ is God, and you are a man. Which one should be believed first?
10.306	Christ has spread His Church over the whole earth. If I say it, you may look down on me. But the Gospel says it, so watch out.
10.307	What does the Gospel say?
10.308	“Christ had to suffer and rise again from the dead on the third day. And repentance and forgiveness of sins had to be preached in His name.”
10.309	Where there is forgiveness of sins, there is the Church.
10.310	How is that the Church? Because it was said to her, “I will give you the keys of the kingdom of heaven. Whatever you set free on earth will be set free in heaven. And whatever you tie up on earth will be tied up in heaven.”
10.311	Where is this forgiveness of sins spread?
10.312	“Through all nations, starting at Jerusalem.”
10.313	So believe Christ! But you know very well that if you believe Christ, you will have nothing to say about “betrayers.” That is why you want me to believe you when you speak evil of my parents. You would rather do that than believe for yourself what Christ predicted!
