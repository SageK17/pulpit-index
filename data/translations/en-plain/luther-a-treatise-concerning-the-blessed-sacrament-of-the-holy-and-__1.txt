0.177	1. First, let us look at the evil practices of the brotherhoods.
0.178	One of them is their gluttony and drunkenness. They hold one or more masses. After that, the whole day and night, and other days as well, are handed over to the devil. They do only what displeases God.
0.179	This mad partying was brought in by the evil spirit. It is called a brotherhood, but it is really a drunken binge. It is a completely heathen way of life. In fact, it is a way of life fit for pigs.
0.180	It would be far better to have no brotherhoods in the world at all than to allow such a detestable thing.
0.181	The worldly rulers and the cities should join with the clergy to get rid of it.
0.182	For it brings great dishonor on God, the saints, and all Christians. And it turns the worship services and the feast days into a game for the devil.
0.183	Saints' days should be kept holy with good works. The brotherhood should also be a special treasure chest of good works. Instead, it has become a treasure chest of beer money.
0.184	What do the names of Our Lady, St. Anne, St. Sebastian, or other saints have to do with your brotherhoods? All you have in them is gluttony, drunkenness, wasting money, howling, yelling, chattering, dancing, and wasting time.
0.185	If a sow were made the patron saint of such a brotherhood, she would not agree to it.
0.186	So why do they cause the dear saints such pain? They misuse the saints' names in these shameful practices and sins. And with such evil practices they dishonor and blaspheme the brotherhoods named after the saints.
0.187	How terrible it will be for those who do this and those who allow it!
0.188	2. If people want to keep a brotherhood going, they should gather food and feed and serve a table or two of poor people, for God's sake. On the day before, they should fast. On the feast day they should stay sober and spend the time in prayer and other good works.
0.189	Then God and His saints would be truly honored. Things would get better, and others would be given a good example.
0.190	Or they should collect the money they planned to waste on drink and put it into a common fund, with each trade having its own. Then fellow workers in need could be helped or lent money. Or a young couple from that trade could be properly provided for from the common fund. These would be true works of brotherhood. They would make God and His saints look on the brotherhoods with pleasure, and the saints would then gladly be their patrons.
0.191	But suppose they are not willing to do this, and they keep following the old foolish show. Then I urge them not to do it on the saints' days, and not in the name of the saints or of the brotherhood.
0.192	Let them choose some other weekday. And let them leave out the names of the saints and of their brotherhoods, so that the saints do not someday show their displeasure.
0.193	There is no day that is not dishonored by such behavior. But at least the festivals and the names of the saints should be spared.
0.194	For such brotherhoods call themselves brotherhoods of the saints, while they do the devil's work.
0.195	3. Another evil in the brotherhoods is spiritual. It is a false opinion they have of themselves. They think their brotherhood should benefit no one except themselves, that is, those who are members, whose names are on the list, or who pay in.
0.196	This damnable, wicked opinion is an even worse evil than the first. It is one of the reasons why God has caused the brotherhoods to become such a mockery and blasphemy of God through gluttony, drunkenness, and the like.
0.197	For there they learn to seek their own good and to love themselves. They learn to be loyal only to one another and to look down on others. They learn to think they are better than others and to imagine that they stand higher before God than others do.
0.198	And so the communion of saints dies out. So do Christian love and the true brotherhood that was set up in the holy sacrament.
0.199	In this way a selfish love grows in them. That is, by means of these many outward brotherhoods based on works, they fight against the one brotherhood of all saints and destroy it. That brotherhood is inner, spiritual, essential, and shared by all.
0.200	When God sees this twisted state of things, He twists it even more. As Psalm 18 says, "With the twisted You will be twisted" [Psalm 18:26]. He causes them to make themselves and their brotherhoods a mockery and a disgrace. He throws them out of the common brotherhood of saints, which they oppose and do not join with. He throws them into their own brotherhood of gluttony, drunkenness, and sexual immorality. So those who have looked for and thought of nothing but what is their own will find what is their own. Then He blinds them, so that they do not see it as something detestable and shameful. Instead, they decorate their disgraceful behavior with the names of saints, as if they were doing right. Beyond this, He lets some of them fall into such a deep pit that they openly boast. They say that anyone in their brotherhood cannot be condemned. It is as if baptism and the sacrament, which God Himself set up, were worth less and were less certain than what they have made up with their darkened minds.
0.201	So their God will bring shame on them and blind them. For with their mad behavior and the piggish practices of their brotherhoods, they mock and blaspheme His feasts, His name, and His saints. This harms the common Christian brotherhood, which flowed from the wounds of Christ.
0.202	4. So if we want to understand and use the brotherhoods rightly, we must learn to tell the difference between brotherhoods correctly.
0.203	The first brotherhood is the divine, heavenly, and noblest one. It is far above all the others, as gold is far above copper or lead. It is the fellowship of all saints, which we talked about above.
0.204	In it we are all brothers and sisters. We are so closely joined that no closer relationship can be imagined. For here we have one baptism, one Christ, one sacrament, one food, one Gospel, one faith, one Spirit, one spiritual body, and each is a member of the other. No other brotherhood is so close.
0.205	Brothers by birth are, of course, brothers of one flesh and blood, of one inheritance and home. But they have to go their separate ways and join themselves to the blood and inheritance of others.
0.206	Organized brotherhoods have one membership list, one mass, one kind of good works, one feast day, one treasury. And as things are now, they also have their common beer, common feast, and common binge. But none of these ties people together so closely that they become one spirit. Only Christ's brotherhood does that.
0.207	The greater, wider, and more all-embracing Christ's brotherhood is, the better it is. So all other brotherhoods should be run in a way that keeps this first and noblest brotherhood always before their eyes. They should consider it alone to be great. In all their works they should seek nothing for themselves. They should do their works for God's sake and ask God to keep this Christian fellowship and brotherhood and make it prosper from day to day.
0.208	So when a brotherhood is formed, the members should show that they are stepping out ahead of others. They should do some special service for Christianity with their prayers, fasting, gifts to the poor, and good works. They should not do it to seek selfish profit or reward, or to shut others out. They should do it to serve as free servants of the whole community of Christians.
0.209	If people understood it rightly like this, God would restore good order. Then the brotherhoods would not be put to shame by drunken partying.
0.210	Then God's blessing would follow. A common fund could be collected, and it could be used to give material help to other people too. Then the spiritual and physical works of the brotherhoods would be done in their proper order.
0.211	If anyone will not follow this way in his brotherhood, my advice is to run from it and leave the brotherhood alone. It will harm him in body and soul.
0.212	But you may say, "If the brotherhood does not give me some special advantage, what good is it to me?"
0.213	I answer: If you are looking for some special advantage, how can the brotherhood or sisterhood help you?
0.214	Use it to serve the community and other people, as love does by its nature. Then you will receive your reward for this love without working for it or wanting it.
0.215	But if you think the service and reward of love are too small, that shows that your brotherhood is a twisted one.
0.216	Love serves freely and for nothing. That is why God also gives every blessing back to love, freely and for nothing.
0.217	Everything must be done in love if it is to please God at all. So the brotherhood also must be a brotherhood in love.
0.218	But what is done in love, by its nature, does not seek its own or its own profit. It seeks the profit of others, and above all of the community.
0.219	5. Let us come back once more to the sacrament. The Christian fellowship is also in bad shape right now, worse than ever before, and it gets worse every day, especially among the rulers. Every place is full of sin and shame. So do not pay attention to how many masses are said or how often the sacrament is celebrated. That will make things worse rather than better. Instead, pay attention to how much you and others are growing in what the sacrament means and in the faith it calls for. Only there can things get better. The more you find that you are being made part of Christ and of the fellowship of His saints, the better off you are. That is, you may find that you are growing strong in your trust in Christ and His dear saints. You may be sure that they love you and stand by you in all the troubles of life and in death. And in turn you may take to heart the failings and falls of all Christians and of the whole Church. Your love may go out to everyone. You may want to help everyone, to hate no one, to suffer with all and pray for them. If so, then the work of the sacrament is going as it should. Then you will often weep, grieve, and mourn over the miserable condition of Christendom today.
0.220	But you may find no such trust in Christ and His saints, and the needs of the Church and of every fellow human being may not trouble or move you. If so, then beware of all other good works, if you think that by doing them you are godly and will be saved.
0.221	You can be sure that they are only hypocrisy, pretense, and deception, or they are without love and fellowship. And without these, nothing is good.
0.222	For the sum of it all is this: Plenitudo legis est dilectio, "Love is what fulfills the law."
0.223	[Romans 13:10] Amen.
