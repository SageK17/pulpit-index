0.213	1 Father of all, whose powerful voice / Called forth this universal frame; / Whose mercies over all rejoice, / Through endless ages still the same. / You, by your word, uphold all things; / Your bounteous love to all is shown, / You hear your every creature's call, / And fill every mouth with good.
0.214	2 In heaven you reign, enthroned in light, / Nature's expanse beneath you spread; / Earth, air, and sea before your sight, / And hell's deep gloom, are open laid. / Wisdom, and might, and love are yours: / Prostrate before your face we fall, / Confess your attributes divine, / And hail the Sovereign Lord of All.
0.215	3 You, sovereign Lord, let all confess / That move in earth, or air, or sky; / Revere your power, your goodness bless, / Tremble before your piercing eye. / All you who owe to Him your birth, / In praise employ your every hour: / Jehovah reigns! Be glad, O earth! / And shout, you morning stars, for joy!
0.216	4 Son of your Father's eternal love, / Take to yourself your mighty power; / Let all earth's sons your mercy know, / Let all adore your bleeding grace. / Display the triumphs of your love; / In every heart reign you alone; / Till all your foes confess your sway, / And glory ends what grace began.
0.217	5 Spirit of grace, and health, and power, / Fountain of light and love below, / Abroad your healing influence shower, / Over all the nations let it flow. / Inflame our hearts with perfect love; / In us the work of faith fulfill; / So heaven's hosts shall move no swifter / Than we on earth to do your will.
0.218	6 Father, it is yours each day to give / Your children's needs a fresh supply: / You clothe the lilies of the field, / And hear the young ravens cry. / On you we cast our care; we live / Through you, who know our every need; / O feed us with your grace, and give / Our souls this day the living bread!
0.219	7 Eternal, spotless Lamb of God, / Before the world's foundation slain, / Sprinkle us ever with your blood; / O cleanse and keep us ever clean. / To every soul (all praise to you!) / Our hearts' compassion move: / And all mankind by this may see / God is in us; for God is love.
0.220	8 Giver and Lord of life, whose power / And guardian care are free to all; / To you, in fierce temptation's hour, / From sin and Satan let us flee. / Yours, Lord, we are, and ours you are; / In us be all your goodness shown; / Renew, enlarge, and fill our heart / With peace, and joy, and heaven, and God.
0.221	9 Blessing and honor, praise and love, / Co-equal, co-eternal Three, / In earth below, in heaven above, / By all your works be paid to you. / Thrice Holy! Yours the kingdom is, / The power omnipotent is yours; / And when created nature dies, / Your never-ceasing glories shine.
