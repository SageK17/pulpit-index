3.129	If God did not spare David, do you think He will spare us if we fall into sin and do not confess and turn from our sins?

3.137	Wash me completely from my iniquity, and cleanse me from my sin.
3.138	For I acknowledge my transgressions, and my sin is continually before me.

3.140	See, I was shaped in iniquity, and my mother conceived me in sin.
3.141	See, you desire truth deep within, and in the hidden part you will teach me wisdom.
3.142	Purge me with hyssop, and I will be clean; wash me, and I will be whiter than snow.
3.143	Cause me to hear joy and gladness, that the bones which you have broken may rejoice.
3.144	Hide your face from my sins, and blot out all my iniquities.
3.145	Create in me a clean heart, O God, and renew a right spirit within me.
3.146	Do not cast me away from your presence, and do not take your Holy Spirit from me.
3.147	Restore to me the joy of your salvation, and uphold me with your free Spirit.
3.148	Then I will teach transgressors your ways, and sinners will be turned back to you.
3.149	Rescue me from bloodguiltiness, O God, you God of my salvation, and my tongue will sing aloud of your righteousness.
3.150	O Lord, open my lips, and my mouth will proclaim your praise.
3.151	For you do not want sacrifice, otherwise I would give it; you find no delight in burnt offering.

3.153	But you say you don’t believe in the Bible.
3.154	Then look at history, and see if this law is not true.
3.155	Maxentine built a false bridge to drown Constantine, but was drowned himself.
3.156	Bajazet was carried about by Tamerlane in an iron cage which he had intended for Tamerlane.
3.157	Maximinus put out the eyes of thousands of Christians; soon after, a fearful disease of the eyes broke out among his people, of which he himself died in great agony.
3.158	Valens caused about eighty Christians to be sent to sea in a ship and burned alive; he was defeated by the Goths and fled to a cottage, where he was burned alive.
3.159	Alexander VI was poisoned by wine he had prepared for another.
3.160	Henry III of France was stabbed in the same chamber where he had helped to plot the cruel massacre of French Protestants.
3.161	Marie Antoinette, riding to Notre Dame Cathedral for her wedding, ordered the soldiers to command all beggars, cripples, and ragged people to leave the route of the procession.
3.162	She could not endure the sight of these miserable ones.
3.163	Soon afterward, bound in the executioner’s cart, she was riding toward the place of execution amid crowds who gazed on her with hearts as cold as ice and hard as granite.
3.164	When Foulon was asked how the starving populace was to live, he said, “Let them eat grass.”
3.165	Afterward the mob, maddened with rage, caught him in the streets of Paris, hanged him, stuck his head on a pike, and filled his mouth with grass.
T:4	A Man Reaps More Than He Sows
4.1	“But other seed fell into good ground and brought forth fruit, some a hundredfold, some sixtyfold, some thirtyfold.”—Matt. 13:8.
4.2	If I sow a bushel, I expect to reap ten or twenty bushels.
4.3	I can sow in one day what it will take ten men to reap.
4.4	The Spaniards have this proverb: “Sow a thought and reap an act.
4.5	Sow an act and reap a habit.
4.6	Sow a habit and reap a character.
4.7	Sow a character and reap a destiny.”
4.8	And it takes a longer time to reap than to sow.
4.9	I have heard of a certain kind of bean that reproduces itself a thousandfold.
4.10	One thistledown that blew from the deck of a ship is said to have covered the entire surface of a South Sea island with thistles.
4.11	The oak springs from an acorn, the mighty Mississippi from a little spring.
4.12	One glass of whisky may lead to a drunkard’s death.
4.13	One lie may ruin a man’s career.
4.14	One error in youth may follow a man all through life.
4.15	Someone has said that many a Christian spends half his time trying to keep down the sprouts of seed sown in his young days.
4.16	Unless it is held in check, the desire to “have a drink” will become a consuming thirst; the desire to “play a game of cards,” an irresistible gambler’s passion.
